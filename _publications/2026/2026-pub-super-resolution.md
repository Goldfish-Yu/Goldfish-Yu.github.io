  We address the information bottleneck in microscopic single-image super-resolution by
  introducing a multi-derived cue framework that leverages complementary signals extracted
  from the input image. The approach significantly enhances the reconstruction of fine
  cellular details, supporting downstream clinical analysis tasks in reproductive medicine
  and beyond.
