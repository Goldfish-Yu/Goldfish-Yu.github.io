abstract: >-
  We propose a direction-free continuous convolution operator for the automated measurement
  of sperm flagella kinematics, removing the dependence on explicit orientation estimation.
  The approach encodes flagella geometry in a continuous representation, enabling robust
  extraction of motility parameters regardless of flagella pose or imaging angle.
